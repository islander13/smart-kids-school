Nyon has a somewhat different profile from the rest of French-speaking Switzerland: sitting between Geneva and Lausanne, the region is home to a large share of international families, often connected to organisations based in Geneva or the area's international schools. Many children here are schooled in English, sometimes alongside French or another language at home. For a coding class, that reality concretely changes what matters most when choosing.

## Why the teaching language deserves to be considered first

A child schooled in English who learns to code in French (or vice versa) adds a layer of mental translation to an exercise that already demands a lot of concentration. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's not neutral either: the right approach is to choose the language your child already thinks school concepts in, so their mental energy goes toward coding logic rather than translation.

## What's available for a child in Nyon

You'll find the same formats as elsewhere: after-school workshops, holiday camps, regular online classes. The difference in Nyon is that local in-person options are often designed for a standard French-speaking audience, leaving few options genuinely built for a child schooled in English or in a different education system (IB, British or American curriculum).
